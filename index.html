Parallel Robotics · Failure Recovery · UBC

Dead skeleton→working 3-DOF robot

Spherical parallel manipulator

Problem
Handed a self-locking robot skeleton: no motors, no electronics, no control system.
What I did
Rebuilt the mechanism on free bearings to remove the binding. Sized and bench-tested new servos (they measured ~60% of rated torque, and the 2.5× design margin absorbed it). Designed a fused 24→12 V power system, redesigned the mounts with FEA and fabricated everything at UBC Makerspaces.
Result
A fully controlled 3-DOF platform with potentiometer, desktop and Android control, IK-driven presets and an MSC Adams model.
  • Root-Cause Analysis
  • Actuator Validation
  • Power Protection
  • FEA
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Inverse Kinematics

Medical Devices · Prosthetics · ISO 13485

$30K+→<$3K

Affordable bionic prosthetic hand

Problem
Multi-grip bionic hands sell for $30K–60K, out of reach for most amputees in cost-sensitive markets.
What I did
Core mechanical designer: custom actuators, a 3D-printed body, CAE-driven design simplification and a patented thumb-swivel mechanism with position feedback, developed to ISO 13485.
Result
Priced under $3K, with 18 grip patterns and ~8 kg load capacity.
  • Custom Actuators
  • Mechanism Design
  • CAE
  • 3D Printing
  • ISO 13485

Indian Patent No. 403148 · Thumb Finger Swiveling System with Position Feedback · granted 2022

Construction Robotics · System Design

$100K of a $1M budget

Cable-driven concrete 3D printer

Problem
Printing house-scale concrete structures normally needs a massive, expensive gantry.
What I did
Designed a cable-driven parallel robot, a custom extruder and vision-based path correction. Went from consultant to project lead of 5 engineers and 2 technicians. Used additive manufacturing and standard parts, with design techniques that got high-quality structures out of basic 3D printers.
Result
Working prototype in 14 months on under $100K of a $1M budget; 8×8 ft concrete prints; targeting 10,000 sq ft structures.
