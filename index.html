02
Parallel Robotics · Failure Recovery · UBC Research

Spherical Parallel Manipulator

Handed a self-locking robot skeleton with no motors, no electronics and no control system. I rebuilt the mechanism on free bearings to remove the binding, sized and bench-tested new servos (measured ~60% of rated torque, which the 2.5× design margin absorbed), designed a fused 24→12 V power system, redesigned the mounts with FEA, and fabricated everything at UBC Makerspaces. The result is a 3-DOF platform with three control modes (potentiometers, a Python desktop GUI and an Android app), IK-driven presets and an MSC Adams model.

Root-Cause Analysis Actuator Validation Power Protection FEA Raspberry Pi / Android Inverse Kinematics / Adams
Dead skeleton → fully controlled 3-DOF platform · Root cause found for every earlier failure · Servo bench-tested at ~60% of rating; design margin covered it.
